# Two-pet exclusive interactions

Status: the three original interactions, the "two-pet relay for deleted icons" and the four new [daily interactions](COMPANION_LIFE.md) are wired in. Versions, verification and release evidence are documented in one place in [Current progress](CHARACTERS.md#current-progress-and-phase-exit-criteria); the actual look of the pet windows is tested by the user.

## Adopted behavior

When "Both Pets Together" is on, idling, curiosity and ordinary wandering/exploring/patrolling can all accept two-person invitations. Except for feeding, both must be on the same monitor and first move to their interaction positions; feeding happens where they stand and can cross monitors. Dragging, cheek pinching, eating, climbing and window attacks are not interrupted by invitations; a sleeping character only accepts the sleep-together invitation. A window Feibi has already stashed in her hat does not block interactions; it stays in its separate storage slot, the existing ownership safety checks keep running during the interaction, and it is not returned automatically. Clicking a character, actively asking for mischief or a window return, turning off coexistence, going home or exiting cleans up the scene. Both characters' life states are saved separately, and scenes never modify real files.

1. **Cheek pinch**: Feibi Jiubi approaches from the right and reaches out to pinch gently; Nuonuo's cheek stretches to the right and then springs softly back. The performance lasts about 2.7 seconds, plus the time to approach.
2. **Sleeping snuggled together**: the two move toward each other, yawn, sit down and lean their heads together; after falling asleep the breathing segment loops, ending about 18.2 seconds after they are in position, and while asleep both characters' sleepiness actually goes down. In Quiet Mode only this interaction is allowed.
3. **Long-range in-place hat removal and treat toss** (2026-09-29): no distance threshold and no approach; height differences and crossing monitors are allowed. The two turn toward each other where they stand; Feibi fully removes her hat, pulls out a snack and throws it at 3.86 seconds, then puts her hat back on. The physical-pixel hand and mouth anchors include the facing, size and DPI transforms; the food stays on its own transparent layer. The flight time adjusts with distance to 0.7–4 seconds, the arc connects different heights, Nuonuo's open-mouth frame 9 is aligned with the arrival moment, and she then keeps chewing instead of ending early because of a long distance. The snack is an apple, cake, donut or pudding; on an actual catch, hunger drops by 9 in normal mode, while Fasting/Infinite Mode leaves hunger unchanged. If the window either of them stands on is moved, hidden or minimized, it aborts rather than letting a character finish the performance in mid-air. The physical gaps between monitors have no picture; the flight logic stays continuous. The maintenance entry points are `MainWindow.PairFeeding.cs` and this project's dedicated `PairFeedFlight`, reusing existing frames, props and transparent windows with no new dependency.

Shared life rhythm adopted 2026-09-28: about 12 seconds after coexistence starts, regular invitations begin, and after each interaction ends they try again 30–55 seconds later; when temporarily busy they retry every 2 seconds without forcibly interrupting protected actions. When either character's sleepiness reaches 70, sleeping together comes first; otherwise, when Nuonuo's hunger reaches 55 and she is not in Fasting/Infinite Mode, feeding comes first; ordinary play keeps alternating cheek pinching and feeding, with ball play mixed in, and when both are a little sleepy they may also sleep together. Meeting, following and watching the fun are triggered separately according to the [daily interaction rules](COMPANION_LIFE.md). After a successful ending, they stay at the end position for about 6 seconds before resuming autonomous activity; user actions and deletion events can cut that stay short. The right-click "Pair Interaction Demo" keeps its original entries and adds the four daily behaviors; scenes do not start when coexistence is off.

Approaching is computed from physical distance and monitor DPI, taking about 0.85–5 seconds. Triggering and rhythm reuse the existing two-window coordination; `PairInteractionPolicy` is only the shared pure rules for this project's invitations, relay and dialogue, maintained by this project. When a scene ends, the coordination state is released first, then the next deletion queue item is handled, so the single-pet flow is not taken by mistake while the previous scene is still ending. Automated regression covers the rules, the production entry points and the real anchors; the desktop effect is accepted by the user.

Interactions that require approaching play the run animation in the actual direction of movement, then return to the interaction artwork's facing on arrival. Size changes, display layout changes, screen lock or sleep abort the scene and clean up props; items of the deletion relay not yet eaten go back into the queue, and items already eaten are not re-queued. Merely clicking the current size without an actual size change does not interrupt the action.

## Two-pet relay for deleted icons

Adopted 2026-09-29: on a hit, the fake icon is closed and counted only once; the coordinated scene keeps holding Nuonuo until the swallowing and chewing of the current `pair_feed_catch` finish (about 2.79 seconds after the action starts), then she goes straight back to idling/the next task. Switching into the single-pet `chomp` slurp animation again is not allowed. Cancellation, dragging or mode switching after a hit must not rebuild an icon that has already been eaten either. Automated tests call the production update/cancel entry points to check continued playback and no fallback after the ghost icon is closed.

Adopted: when Nuonuo receives a deletion event with a precise desktop position, both pets are together and both are available, Feibi Jiubi preferably runs to the right of that icon and kicks it out, and Nuonuo catches it with her mouth open on the other side; when the icon is at the left edge of the monitor and there is no room for Nuonuo on the left, the icon first hits the left edge of the screen and then bounces into her mouth on the right. Both align to the physical-pixel coordinates of the same monitor, and frame timing and running distance are computed from the monitor size. An icon at the top can drop slightly during the wind-up to a fully visible foot-contact height; before the kick the fake icon and its text are still a single in-place visual, and on the kick the text fades out and the icon flies on its own. Only a hit on the mouth calls the original one-time eating counter, followed by chewing. Feibi gains no hunger value or meal count.

Real files are still deleted only by Windows; the relay reuses the existing deletion queue and the same fake-icon window, never creates a second icon, and batch deletions are processed one at a time. Feibi walking/being curious and items in her hat no longer block the relay; if she is landing or waking up, the current icon is kept and it waits at most 5 seconds, and if she is asleep she is woken first, and the two set off together once ready. No new autonomous window attack starts while waiting; on timeout, or when she is being dragged/cheek-pinched/attacking a window, on a different monitor, without a precise position, genuinely without enough space, or when the icon is no longer valid, it falls back to Nuonuo eating alone, the interrupted item and the original fake icon go back to the front of the queue, and an 8-second cooldown prevents waiting on the same item repeatedly. Quiet Mode and turning off deletion responses abort the relay. `PairIconKickWaiting/Started/Impact/Ended/Skipped` record the queue number, state and fallback reason without recording icon content; the old early-exit path no longer fails silently. The new coordination only serves this project's two-window interaction and is not abstracted into a separate general system.

Edge positioning uses the real foot and mouth markers of the current official frames and the character size. The icon and text stay in place while waiting and while the characters run over; when both are in position and winding up, icons at any of the four edges can slide slightly to a fully visible kicking position, so icons in the first column are not rejected just because of the transparent margin around the feet. Background tests cover positioning at all four corners on negative-coordinate monitors, at 60%/100%/200% sizes and at 100%/125%/150% DPI.

## Speech bubbles above the head

Adopted: "Show Speech Bubbles" in the right-click menu is a switch shared by both pets and saved across launches; when old settings lack the field it defaults to off. When on, each character responds to some state changes with her own short lines, and occasionally says something when idle alone; when both pets are together and both available, they take turns showing one line each in their own dialogues, and "Pair Interaction Demo → Let Them Chat" in the right-click menu can also request it. A manual request makes ordinary wandering stop and wakes a sleeping character, and waits at most 30 seconds when one is busy eating or making mischief. Each round lasts about 5.7 seconds, and both characters' autonomous actions are held off while they talk; dragging, window mischief, existing two-person actions and safety states can still interrupt. The bubble is a separate transparent window that the mouse clicks through and that never takes focus; it follows the character and is positioned by the monitor work area and DPI. Quiet Mode hides the bubbles; "Mute" only controls sound and does not change the text switch. The test edition's launcher does not show this switch, and bubbles do not appear during the automated demonstration.

## Art and maintenance basis

The life-rhythm and deletion-relay fixes in this round reuse the selected assets below, without regenerating or iterating on generated images; what changed are the invitation rules, scene transitions and real anchor positioning. Two-person dialogue also lets ordinary walking stop to reply, and items in the hat no longer block talking; the speech-bubble switch and the mute/Quiet settings keep their original constraints.

Every character action sheet was generated directly from that character's **original character image**, and no generated image or cropped frame was fed back into a generation model; for the prompts and rules of the new daily actions, see [Two-pet daily interactions](COMPANION_LIFE.md):

- Feibi Jiubi original: `assets/characters/feibijiubi/original.png`
- Fu Nuonuo original: `assets/characters/nuonuo/original.png`
- The six selected action sheets generated directly from the originals: `assets/pair_interactions/sheets/`. Feeding uses `feed_throw_feibi.png` and `feed_catch_nuonuo_upright.png`; earlier feeding drafts are kept as historical assets and are no longer used at runtime.
- Deterministic cropping script: `tools/process_pair_sprites.py`. It splits along the 16 complete character outlines of the original sheet, never cutting off hat tops, hair or feet that cross the equal-division grid lines. Each action set is calibrated against the same character's idle neutral face width; Feibi's treat toss after removing her hat uses the last hat-removal frame as reference. The whole set shares one scale factor and is positioned by the foot touching the ground, never re-centered because reaching hands, hair tips or the hat change the bounding box.
- 16-frame transparent runtime sequences: each character's own `runtime/pair_cheek`, `runtime/pair_sleep`, `runtime/pair_feed`
- At runtime, Nuonuo's feeding frames read the same `runtime/pair_feed` folder in three beats: `pair_feed_wait` (frames 0–2 looping), `pair_feed_ready` (frames 3–5) and `pair_feed_catch` (frames 6–15), without copying or regenerating images; the food's landing point is re-aligned to the new front-facing open-mouth frames.
- The complete hat removal/putting the hat back on directly reuse Feibi Jiubi's existing `runtime/hat_open` and `runtime/hat_wear`; the new `pair_feed` frames only cover reaching in and throwing after the hat is off.
- Frame hashes and source records: `assets/pair_interactions/previews/pair-sprite-manifest.json`
- `assets/pair_interactions/calibration.json` stores the per-frame affine mapping of the annotated hand, hat-opening and mouth coordinates onto the new canvas. Official feeding, the deleted-icon relay and the offline previews all read it, so the old landing points cannot keep being used after the size calibration. It only serves the two existing two-person eating paths and does not establish a public extension protocol.
- The relay icon kick adds `icon_kick_feibi.png`, regenerated with direct reference to Feibi's original and split into the 16 frames of `runtime/pair_icon_kick`; Nuonuo reuses the `pair_feed_wait/ready/catch` open-mouth catching frames, likewise generated directly from her own original. The separate fake-icon layer is not part of the generated action sheet.
- Generation inputs and action timelines: `docs/PAIR_SPRITE_PROMPTS.md`
- The four small food images are drawn by `tools/generate_pair_treats.py`; they are not re-edits of a generated action sheet.

If the art needs adjusting later, go back to the original character images to regenerate the whole action sheet, then run the deterministic cropping script; do not use the current generated sheets as image-model input. The two-pet interaction is only a local coordination layer on this project's existing `MainWindow` two-window architecture, with no cross-project framework. The original single-pet icon eating and real-window mischief logic are not taken over by the interaction layer.

Adopted 2026-09-28: after the two characters' ordinary walking/running were re-cropped from the originally selected action sheets, a deterministic translation registration is applied using the median horizontal face position, keeping the feet, vertical stride and original poses unchanged. The reason is that the old version centered on the whole outline, so hair tips and extended legs changing the outline width caused extra horizontal jitter. The implementation reuses the existing Pillow/OpenCV processing tools, adds no dependency, and is maintained by this project. `tools/audit_motion_continuity.py` checks that the horizontal anchor range of movement loops is no more than 1.5 asset pixels, and that the face width of the two-person neutral poses deviates from idle by less than 10%; width changes from turning the head, crouching or stretching the face are not counted as size faults.

## Previews and user testing

Offline asset previews of cheek pinching and sleeping together use `tools/pair_interaction_demo.py`. The current feeding preview first uses `--feed-preview` of the background logic tests to export the production `PairFeedFlight` and `SpriteAnimator` trajectories, then uses `tools/remote_feed_preview.py` to compose `dist/pair-interaction-demo/treat-toss-in-place-long.gif` and `treat-toss-in-place-reverse-height.gif`, checking long distance, swapped left/right positions and height differences respectively. The old fixed-position feeding preview no longer represents current behavior. None of the previews start the desktop pet, open windows or play sound; real monitor DPI, window layering and manual interruptions still need the user's confirmation.

The user runs the current source, turns on "Both Pets Together", right-clicks either of them and chooses the three items of "Pair Interaction Demo" in turn. Observe: during the cheek pinch, whether hand and face make contact; while sleeping, whether the two lean together without missing head tops or feet; during feeding, whether Feibi fully removes her hat, whether the food appears from the hat and flies a clear distance, whether Nuonuo opens her mouth wide before it arrives, and whether Feibi puts her hat back on. Repeat feeding to confirm the four random foods. Check that normal idling resumes in Quiet Mode, after drag interruption, with coexistence off and on monitors with different scaling. If anything is wrong, reporting the time range of the latest diagnostic log under `%LOCALAPPDATA%/SoftMochiPet/` is enough to locate it.
