JiuNuo Desktop Pet v1.3.3 · Portable Edition · 2026-10-01

What's new: per-clip voice loudness balancing for both characters; kicking an icon to Nuonuo no longer repeats the solo slurp; mouse cheek pinching now has continuous stretching, squinting and rebound; feeding is an in-place toss and catch that no longer requires the two to be close, and allows height differences and crossing monitors.

How to use
1. First extract the whole archive to an ordinary folder; do not run it directly from the archive preview window.
2. Double-click "JiuNuoDesktopPet.exe", the only normal launcher in the root folder. You do not need to install .NET or any other runtime.
3. Right-click the pet or the system tray icon and choose Nuonuo or Feibi Jiubi under "Pet Character"; turn on "Both Pets Together" to have both of them appear. Size, mute and Quiet Mode are shared; while both are together, turning on Fasting Mode or Infinite Mode hides both the hunger bar and the mischief bar, and they only come back when both options are off. Fasting Mode still only changes Nuonuo's hunger behavior and does not clear Feibi's mischief value. "Show Speech Bubbles" controls the text bubbles, and two-person dialogue is also supported while both are together.
4. Feibi Jiubi has "Allow Autonomous Mischief" on by default. If your old settings version was 4 or earlier, the first upgrade turns it on once; if you then turn it off from the menu, it stays off after restarting, and the bar above her head turns gray. The right-click "Mischief status" explains charging, cooldown, waiting for a window, or an occupied hat.
5. You can first try "Make Mischief Now" and "Tease Her" with an ordinary blank test window. The hat holds at most one window; while it is holding one she can still kick, punch or charge another compatible window, but she will not stash a second window or start a new hat special move. After a special move, the shards or two halves stashed in the hat are not restored automatically; right-click "Give the Windows Back" first stops the current small action and then returns the original window.
6. To exit, right-click the pet or its system tray icon and choose "Exit Desktop Pet".

New interactions
- Hold the left mouse button on a cheek and drag outward or diagonally: the cheek stretches continuously and she squints; pulling far and holding makes her briefly open her mouth, and releasing makes it spring back. Her body tilts slightly and her size does not change. Both characters support left and right cheeks; dragging from the hat or body still lifts and throws her as before.
- Feeding in the right-click "Pair Interaction Demo" now happens where the two are standing, without requiring them to come close; it supports standing on either side, different heights and crossing screens, and the farther apart they are, the longer the food flies. Feeding still does not start while in Quiet Mode, while being dragged, or while busy attacking.
- While both are together, they start inviting each other to interact after about 12 seconds; after each interaction ends they try again 30–55 seconds later, delaying if busy. If Nuonuo is hungry, feeding comes first; if one of them is sleepy, sleeping snuggled together comes first; when idle they also play ball, pinch cheeks and toss treats. You can also trigger these yourself from the right-click menu.
- New: "Little Tagalong", "Nuzzle in Passing", "Play Ball Together" and "Watch the Fun Together". If one walks far away the other follows; when they meet they may nuzzle; when a window opens or moves nearby they may watch it together; the ball comes out of the hat, gets kicked back and forth, and is finally put away.
- The right-click "Pair Interaction Demo" can trigger each of these four behaviors. First turn off Quiet Mode and have both stand on the same screen on the same stretch of ground or the same window ledge; playing ball needs a fairly wide space. Watching the fun needs a visible window on that screen. These four interactions give way to icon deletion, dragging or active mischief, and do not change real windows or files.
- When a desktop icon is deleted, on the same screen Feibi preferably kicks it to Nuonuo; walking or a window held in the hat no longer prevents the relay. If Feibi is landing or waking up, it waits at most 5 seconds; if she is asleep she is woken first. While dragging or attacking, on a different screen, or when positions cannot be located accurately, Nuonuo still eats it alone. Use a newly created temporary file to try this.
- Changing size, turning off coexistence, locking the screen or sleeping the PC stops the current two-person interaction, so characters and food never end up misaligned.

Notes
- "JiuNuoDesktopPet.exe" in the root folder is the launcher; the "app" subfolder contains the animations, voices and the bundled .NET runtime. Keep the two together.
- Do not move, rename or copy anything inside "app" separately.
- The Exclusive Feature Test launcher is not included in the release package; the release package only provides "JiuNuoDesktopPet.exe" in the root folder and the "app" runtime folder.
- This is the official Windows 10/11 x64 Portable Edition; window interactions and how the voices sound still need to be checked on your own monitors and apps.
- After a window has been stashed, Quiet Mode, going home, resizing and dragging across screens do not trigger a restore; switching characters, exiting, locking the screen, sleeping the PC, or a real change in display layout or work area returns it safely, so no window is left behind.
- While the character or tray menu is open, the character temporarily stops walking and animating, and continues once the menu closes. Submenus, the size slider and window safety checks keep working.
- Before updating, exit the old version from the tray first, then extract the new one to a new folder and start it, so the old process cannot take over the launch request.
- If Windows shows a security prompt because the publisher is unknown, first make sure the file came from someone you trust. This version is not signed with a purchased code-signing certificate.
- The official rename only changes the product name and release file names; the characters are still called Nuonuo and Feibi Jiubi, and old settings, volume and save data stay in the original SoftMochiPet data folder, with no manual migration needed.
- To upgrade, extract the complete package to a new folder; do not replace only the EXE or mix old and new "app" folders. If you want it to start with Windows, re-check "Start with Windows" in the new version's menu to update the existing startup entry to point to the new launcher.
